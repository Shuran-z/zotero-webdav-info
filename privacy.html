Privacy policy

The integration uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to store, list, update, and retrieve Zotero attachment archives and their sync metadata in Google Drive. It uses that data only for attachment synchronization and checks needed to operate the sync.

Google Drive stores the cloud copies. The Mac retains local attachment files, temporary transfer data, and the Google OAuth credential used by rclone. Attachment data and credentials are not sent to this website. This site has no forms, analytics scripts, or application cookies. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, handles web requests under its own privacy policy.

The operator can revoke Google access from their Google Account, remove the local rclone credential, and manage or delete cloud copies through Google Drive.
