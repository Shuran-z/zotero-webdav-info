Zotero WebDAV Mac

Zotero WebDAV Mac is a personal integration that lets Zotero send and retrieve attachments from the operator’s Google Drive through an rclone WebDAV endpoint running on the same Mac. Zotero manages bibliographic records and notes through its own sync service.

This site describes the integration. It does not run the integration or offer an account or sign-in.
